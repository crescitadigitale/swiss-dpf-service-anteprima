You may request access to, rectification or deletion of your data, object to the processing and request portability, within the limits provided by law. Write to contatti@swissdpfservice.ch. 8. External links and map

The site contains links to map services (Google Maps, Apple Maps). When you open them you leave our site and those services’ own notices apply.

The map of garages under agreement uses swisstopo maps (Swiss Confederation) and loads only after you press “Show the map”. At that moment your browser requests the map images from swisstopo’s servers, which receive your IP address. We do not use cookies.

In the garage form, the search in the business register (UID register of the Confederation) is optional and starts while you type the name or UID number, after a short pause, or with the search button: the text you type is sent to the federal register, which receives your IP address. We do not store it.

9. Data of companies and garages

To propose collaborations we collect the contact details of companies and garages (name, address, phone, e-mail, UID number) from public sources, such as the commercial register, or directly from the persons concerned. We use them only to assess and manage a possible agreement and to contact those who have asked or agreed to be contacted. A garage appears on the site only with its written consent. You may ask at any time not to be contacted any more or to be removed: write to contatti@swissdpfservice.ch.
