About me

Behind SWISS DPF SERVICE there is a person.

I worked as an employee in the technical and commercial management of companies in the construction sector and, for five years, as head of human resources. The desire to do something for myself, in the world of services, led me down this path.

I started from a personal experience and deepened my technical and mechanical knowledge with the help of industry experts. In this way I came to identify solutions that are cheaper and more eco-friendly than replacing the filter. This is how the project was born, becoming reality after a year and a half of study and testing.

Our specialisation

Our work starts with the filter.

We specialise in particulate filter systems for diesel and petrol vehicles, professional filter regeneration and preventive engine decarbonisation.

We are not a general car garage: our focus is a single field, and we treat it with attention to the system and its condition.
