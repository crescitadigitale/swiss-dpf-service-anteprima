What it is

The service in plain words.

GPF (Gasoline Particulate Filter) and OPF (Otto Particulate Filter) are the particulate filters of petrol engines, found on many recent vehicles. As with diesels, they trap the particles produced by combustion and can become clogged over time.

When to contact us

Typical signs and situations

  • Engine warning light on
  • Loss of power or irregular running
  • Faults reported by the vehicle related to exhaust gases

Frequently asked questions

The most requested answers.

What is the difference between DPF/FAP and GPF/OPF?

DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) and FAP (Filtre à Particules) both refer to the particulate filter of diesel engines. GPF (Gasoline Particulate Filter) and OPF (Otto Particulate Filter) are the particulate filters of petrol engines.

Can a filter always be regenerated?

No. Suitability depends on the condition of the filter and on the specific case: a damaged filter, for example one with cracks, may not be regenerable. That is why we assess each case before confirming the work.

How much does the service cost and how long does it take?

It depends on the type of filter and the service requested. Describe your vehicle and the problem: we will reply with a dedicated assessment and quote.

Beyond the filter

Decarbonisation of the engine assembly.

For petrol engines, decarbonisation of the engine assembly, that is, treatment of carbon deposits, can also be considered. It is decided case by case, depending on the vehicle and its condition.

Diesel and petrol: the same service applies to both types of engine. We do not promise results in terms of consumption, emissions or performance.
